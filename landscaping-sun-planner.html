Which way is north?

Shadows are only right if north is right. Tell the planner which direction the top edge of your photo points — drag the compass on the photo, use the slider, or read it off your phone.

0° N The top of the photo points due north.

Read it from your phone

Stand in the yard, hold your phone flat, and point its top edge the same way the top of your photo points — toward the back fence, say. Then set north from the phone.

Compass is off. Phone is pointing: —

Phone compasses find magnetic north; true north sits a few degrees off, depending on where you live. Add your local declination here, or skip it and nudge the slider until a shadow in the photo lines up with the shadow on screen.
