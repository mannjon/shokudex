SHOKUDEX — HOW TO PUT THIS ON GITHUB PAGES

What this is: the Shokudex page, as a plain website.
All the files belong together, side by side — not in a folder.

UPLOAD STEPS

1. Unzip this file on your computer or phone. You will see
   a pile of loose files, starting with index.html.

2. On github.com, make a new repository (or open the one
   you want to use for Shokudex).

3. In that repository, choose Add file, then Upload files.
   Upload EVERY file from the unzipped pile, just as they
   are — all of them at the top level of the repository,
   not inside a folder. Commit the changes.

4. In the repository, open Settings, then Pages.
   Under "Build and deployment", choose:
   Source: Deploy from a branch
   Branch: main, folder: / (root)
   Save.

5. Wait a minute or two. GitHub shows your site's address
   at the top of that same Pages settings page.
   It will look like: yourname.github.io/repo-name

PUT IT ON YOUR HOME SCREEN

1. Open that address in Chrome on your phone.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
4. From then on, open Shokudex from its own icon.
   No Muse bar on top, and no pull-to-reload wrapped
   around the card.
